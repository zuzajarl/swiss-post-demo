# Access points and digital services

> Which service is available at which kind of access point, and which digital route
> settles an errand with no counter visit at all.

---

---

## Partner branch

The postal counter sits inside an existing shop — Coop, Volg, Landi, Denner or a local
store. The partner's staff are trained for postal business.

For the caller the point that matters is this: hours are mostly longer than before, there
is no lunchtime closure, and Saturday is often staffed all day.

---

## Parcel pickup point

Staffed point for parcels. Typically weekdays from half past six to seven in the evening,
Saturday mornings. No cash, no payments.

---

## My Post 24

Terminal accessible around the clock, including public holidays. Drop off and collect
parcels. Collection uses a code sent when the item arrives.

---

## Home service

The carrier handles, at the front door: accepting items, selling stamps, delivering cash,
taking payments.

**Free of charge** for people with no access point within reasonable distance. This is the
standard answer for anyone who is not mobile, whether for health reasons or lack of
transport.

---

## Digital services

### Post App
Track every shipment in real time, redirect or hold parcels, buy digital stamps, authorise
registered items, set a drop-off location.
→ Replaces a counter visit for: tracking, parcel receipt, stamps, redirection, holding.

### WebStamp / digital stamp
Buy stamps online and print them yourself, or get them as an SMS code to write by hand. The
SMS code works without a printer.
→ Replaces a counter visit for: buying stamps, sending letters.

### E-Post / digital letter
Physical letters are digitised and delivered in the app. Outgoing letters can be submitted
online, then printed and posted by Swiss Post.
→ Replaces a counter visit for: sending letters.

### Online redirection order
Redirection and change of address entirely online. Only needed when moving house, **not**
because of a branch closure.
→ Replaces a counter visit for: redirection, change of address.

### eBill / E-Finance
Receive and pay bills digitally. Counter payments remain available at partner branches.
→ Replaces a counter visit for: paying bills.

### PickPost
Have items delivered to a collection point of your choice instead of your home. Useful when
the usual branch has closed and another point suits better.
→ Does not replace a counter visit, but moves it somewhere more convenient.

## Mapping errand → digital route

| Errand | First recommendation | Second option |
|---|---|---|
| Send a letter | E-Post / digital letter | WebStamp, home service |
| Send a parcel | My Post 24 | Home service, partner branch |
| Receive a parcel | Post App (drop-off, redirect) | My Post 24, PickPost |
| Track a shipment | Post App | — |
| Buy stamps | Post App | WebStamp, home service |
| Pay a bill | eBill / E-Finance | Home service, partner branch |
| Redirection | Online redirection order | — |
| Change of address | Online redirection order | — |
| Withdraw cash | Home service | Partner branch |
| Collect a registered item | Post App (authorisation) | Partner branch, parcel pickup |

**Important:** the digital route is offered, never pushed. Anyone who wants a counter gets
an address.
